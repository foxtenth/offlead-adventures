Behind Offlead Aventures - Our story

A Passion Built on Experience

Bringing a professional, caring approach to canine enrichment in Lisbon.

From the UK to Lisbon's Trails

After 20 years living in the UK and running a successful dog grooming salon, Off Lead Adventures was created to bring a professional, caring approach to dog enrichment.

Years of working closely with dogs and their owners have taught us that every dog is different. Some need confidence, some need stimulation, and some simply need the chance to be a dog.

Our goal is simple: Give dogs amazing experiences while giving owners complete peace of mind.

Selective & Safe

We welcome friendly, suitable dogs. By strictly capping our groups at 4 dogs maximum, we ensure close supervision and harmonious pack chemistry.

True Enrichment

We look beyond basic physical exhaustion. Our adventures focus on mental stimulation, nature exploration, and safe off-lead freedom.

The Complete Care

From our secure Renault Kangoo transport out of Benfica, Carnide, Telheiras, and Laranjeiras to our post-adventure paw rinse, we handle all the details.
