The Ultimate Off-Lead Day Out

Independent and tailored outdoor adventures for dogs in Lisbon.
Off Lead Adventures provides small-group outdoor experiences for dogs who need more than a quick trip around the block. Your dog is collected from your home, joins a carefully managed adventure in nature, enjoys safe exploration, socialisation and enrichment then returns home refreshed, happy and ready to relax.

Not Another Dog Walk

At Offlead Adventures, we believe dogs are happiest when they are exploring the great outdoors.
A quick walk around the block isn't always enough.

As dedicated canine tour guides, we pick up your dog and take them to safe, expansive environments - from rolling green hills to secluded woodlands.
They spend hours running off-lead in a supervised, small-pack environment, allowing them to truly act like dogs.

What We Offer

Wilderness Day Tours

Full-day excursions to safe, rural locations. We explore forests, hills, and safe water spots, giving your dog hours of off-lead freedom.

Pack Socialisation

We carefully curate small groups of compatible dogs. Your dog will build confidence, learn healthy play, and make lifelong furry friends.

Pet Taxi & Door-to-Door Chauffeur

Take dogs to vet appointments, to restaurants or anywhere you wish. One to One.

You don't have to lift a finger. We collect your dog in our safe, air-conditioned vehicle in the morning and return them happy and tired.
